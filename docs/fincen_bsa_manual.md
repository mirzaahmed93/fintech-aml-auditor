# FinCEN Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Final Rule (31 CFR § 1010.230)

## Third-Party Payment Risk
Automated payment reconciliation systems must verify that the originator of a wire transfer (bank remitter) matches the invoiced entity. Auto-reconciling mismatched names without manual review facilitates Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML) via third-party shell companies.

Any fuzzy matching threshold below 90% strict name matching MUST require human-in-the-loop review.
